PERFORMANCE REPORT ON INTERVENTION AT DAR-UL-
         AMAN, MANDI BAHAUDDIN.PUNJAB

1. Introduction
Human Rights Protection Foundation (HRPF) remains steadfast in its
mission to uphold human dignity, promote transparency, and ensure the
protection of fundamental rights across Pakistan. Despite operating with
limited resources, HRPF continues to address public grievances and
advocate for vulnerable communities through lawful and institutional
mechanisms.
This report outlines HRPF’s recent intervention concerning serious
concerns at Dar-ul-Aman, Mandi Bahauddin.
2. Background of the Issue
HRPF received credible information from various sources indicating that
Dar-ul-Aman, Mandi Bahauddin—a government shelter home established
to provide protection and rehabilitation to vulnerable women—was
facing severe deficiencies in basic facilities.
The reported issues included:
Lack of proper sanitation and hygiene facilities
Inadequate healthcare arrangements
Poor cleanliness standards
Negligence by responsible local authorities
Absence of proper monitoring and accountability mechanisms
Such conditions were not only alarming but also constituted a violation of
the fundamental rights and dignity of the residents.
3. HRPF’s Legal and Institutional Action
Recognizing the gravity of the matter, HRPF promptly initiated formal
action. A detailed complaint was submitted to the Office of the Provincial
Ombudsman Punjab, highlighting:
The urgent need for inspection
Immediate rectification of deficiencies
Administrative accountability
Protection of the fundamental rights of
women residing in the facility
The application emphasized that Dar-ul-Aman residents are among the
most vulnerable segments of society and deserve protection, dignity, and
access to basic necessities as guaranteed under constitutional and human
rights frameworks.
4. Outcome and Improvements
Following HRPF’s intervention and engagement with the Provincial
Ombudsman’s office:
Official attention was drawn to the situation.
Administrative inquiries were initiated.

HRPF’S GREAT EFFORTS AGAINST HUMAN SMUGGLING:
DETAILED REPORT ON HUMAN TRAFFICKING FROM MANDI
               BAHAUDDIN, PAKISTAN

Introduction
Human trafficking has reached alarming levels in Mandi Bahauddin,
Pakistan. Hundreds of young men have lost their lives in illegal migration
attempts, often through dangerous border crossings and boat
journeys, while thousands remain detained in foreign prisons under harsh
conditions.
The Human Rights Protection Foundation (HRPF) has emerged as a critical
institution in combating human smuggling and trafficking. Through
investigative journalism, advocacy, and pressure on authorities, HRPF has
exposed organized criminal networks and demanded justice for victims—
even while its Chairman has faced direct threats from human smuggling
mafias due to his unwavering efforts.

HRPF CONCERNS ABOUT CONTAMINATION OF THE CANALES FRESH-WATER IN Mandi BAHAUDDIN
The Human Rights Protection Foundation has expressed its deep concern to the Secretary of Environment Punjab and the Director General of Environment regarding the contamination of the canal's freshwater in Mandi Bahauddin. This pollution is caused by the discharge of sewage into the canal and the disposal of dead chicks by poultry farm owners. In response, the Deputy Director of the I&S Department, Punjab Environment Department, directed the Assistant Director of Environment in Mandi Bahauddin to address the issue immediately. Following this directive, the Assistant Director formally informed senior officials about the situation, and show-cause notices were issued to approximately 136 poultry farm owners across the district. Additionally, some cases were sent to the Punjab Environmental Tribunal.
Furthermore, social and political figure Muhammad Arshad Lilla recently shared videos exposing how the TMA Mandi Bahauddin is worsening environmental pollution by dumping sewage and rotten city waste into the freshwater canal. In response, the Human Rights Protection Foundation once again raised the matter with senior 
authorities and subsequently filed a case with the Provincial Ombudsman Regional Office in Mandi Bahauddin. The case is currently being pursued with detailed documentation.

By the grace of Allah, we are fully committed to serving the public interest, upholding human rights, and ensuring transparency in institutions. We will leave no stone 
unturned in addressing this critical issue.

INSTITUTIONAL ACCOUNTABILITY IN ACTION: PROVINCIAL
 OMBUDSMAN PUNJAB RESPONDS TO HRPF ’s PETITION
 ON GOVERNMENT PRIMARY SCHOOL MIANWAL RANJHA.

Human Rights Protection Foundation (HRPF) remains steadfast in its
mission to uphold human rights, promote transparency, and strengthen
institutional accountability across Pakistan. Throughout the year, HRPF has
continued its dedicated efforts to ensure that fundamental rights are
protected and that public institutions fulfill their constitutional
responsibilities.
We firmly believe that access to quality education is a fundamental human
right. The condition of public educational institutions
reflects the State’s commitment to safeguarding this right. In line with this
belief, HRPF formally submitted a petition to the Honorable Provincial
Ombudsman Punjab concerning the deteriorating and unsafe condition of
Government Primary School Mianwal Ranjha.
The school was facing serious infrastructural deficiencies, lack of essential
facilities, and prolonged neglect, which raised grave concerns among
students, parents, and the local community. Recognizing the urgency of the
matter, the respected Provincial Ombudsman Punjab took notice of the
complaint and initiated necessary measures to address the issues and
improve the condition of the school.
This constructive response underscores the importance of institutional
accountability and demonstrates the positive impact of lawful civic
engagement. It reaffirms that when civil society organizations responsibly
highlight
governance gaps, corrective action becomes possible.
HRPF remains committed to serving humanity through peaceful, lawful, and
effective advocacy. We will continue to raise our voice for marginalized
communities, monitor public service delivery, and work tirelessly to protect
and promote fundamental human rights across the country.
